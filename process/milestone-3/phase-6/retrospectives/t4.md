## Checklist

1. Main learning:
   - The `IrMember` migration is workable when widget-only traversals are
     explicit (`IrNode::widget_children()`), while semantic control-flow
     sites dispatch on `IrMember`. That kept the schema change, compiler
     emit/lower, runtime textual IR parser, validation, and static
     presence reducer in one buildable unit.
   - The review follow-up showed that this was not enough: introducing a
     new tree/member variant creates a semantic migration, not just a type
     migration. Every traversal must be classified by contract before the
     task can truthfully claim closure: widget-only invariant, declared
     member order, materialised child order, positional metadata, or
     validation/defense-in-depth. A helper such as `widget_children()` is a
     proof obligation, not a proof; each use must have a reason why
     dropping `ControlFlow` is intentional and tested. This lesson carries
     directly into T5's reactive positional insertion, T7/T8's Visual-tree
     evidence, and any later phase that adds `else` / `switch` / iteration
     or retained keyed subtrees.
   - Retrospective item 10 should treat implementation-level invariants as
     cross-task constraints when later tasks can step on them. The original
     "no new ADR-level constraint" framing missed that T5/T7 could be
     broken by traversal and positional-metadata choices even without an
     ADR change. For later tasks / phases / milestones, "not an ADR
     change" must not imply "no carry-forward": if a future owner must
     preserve an ordering, identity, validator, or materialisation rule,
     record it as carry-forward with evidence and a re-trigger criterion.
